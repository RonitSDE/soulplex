# Soulplex · five-minute founder demo

## Say this first

Soulplex helps people discover a connection through shared experiences. You build a personal AI representative by talking about your life and approving what it remembers. Representatives explore a short scene in our virtual town, then the two people add their own perspective and decide whether to meet. The product is designed to measure real introductions and representation accuracy, not minutes spent swiping.

This is an independent, pre-traction project. All demo identities are fictional. No existing KULT users, revenue or registrations are presented as Soulplex traction.

## Walkthrough

1. **0:00–0:40 — The world.** Open localhost:8000 and sign in as Maya. Rotate the Three.js town and switch among the café, bookshop and garden. Explain that these are conversation contexts, not entertainment rewards that keep people from meeting.
2. **0:40–1:30 — The personal story.** Open My story. Enter a fictional answer: “Moving to Dubai taught me to build friendships slowly. On weekends I enjoy cooking with a small group.” Send it. Explain that demo interviewing is deterministic; live mode uses the configured model.
3. **1:30–2:10 — Inspectable memory.** Open My memory. Show the new private proposal and its source quote. Correct it if needed, choose “My agent may share this,” then Save & approve. Show that other private details remain private.
4. **2:10–3:00 — Representatives explore.** Open The town, select a venue and explore a moment with Arjun. With the worker running, the queued scene completes. Read two turns and expand the evidence. These are simulated representative statements, not evidence of actual human chemistry.
5. **3:00–4:00 — People take over.** As Maya, write an original contribution, check the review box and choose “I’d like to meet.” Contact is still hidden. Switch to Arjun, open Our moments, choose Maya's scene, and contribute independently. Accept to reveal the mutually chosen contact methods. A video call or café booking has not been made automatically.
6. **4:00–4:30 — Control remains real.** Switch back to Maya, make one of her approved memories private, then revisit the scene. Earlier output is hidden and the introduction needs a fresh scene. Alternatively, demonstrate a block.
7. **4:30–5:00 — Show the experiment.** Open Operator. Explain which events the product captures and the next cohort experiment. Demo activity is explicitly labelled and cannot be described as customer traction.

Use separate browser profiles for a two-person live staging demo. The one-click switcher is a fictional demo convenience and is unavailable in live mode.

## What the investor should learn

- Working software exists across frontend, database, API and a separate agent worker.
- The team can demonstrate provenance and user control over personal representation.
- The key hypothesis remains unproven: a representative-led scene plus a human shared prompt can create better introductions with less effort than conventional profile discovery.
- The fundable milestone is evidence from a small consenting local cohort, not a compatibility percentage generated by another model.

## Measurement sheet for the pilot

Track unique people and unique pairs; do not count repeated clicks as new demand. Segment by city and mutually eligible pool size. The built-in dashboard contains basic activity counts; the richer funnel below needs an analysis query or instrumentation extension before reporting.

| Metric                  | Definition                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Activation              | Approved shareable memory + verified eligibility + member opted into discovery / consented sign-ups          |
| Representation accuracy | Members answering yes to “My representative described me accurately” / members answering that question       |
| Human participation     | Pairs with both human contributions / completed current scenes                                               |
| Mutual invitation       | Unique pairs with both accepts / pairs with both contributions                                               |
| Real conversation       | Unique invited pairs where a participant reports a conversation occurred; show whether one or both confirmed |
| Second-date interest    | Per-person yes / people answering after a real conversation; report missing responses separately             |
| Cost per introduction   | Provider invoices + allocated hosting/support costs / unique mutually accepted pairs                         |
| Time saved              | A separately consented user-reported baseline and follow-up, not a claim inferred from shorter sessions      |

## Funding story and next proof

Start one sufficiently dense local pool. Dubai and Delhi are operational options, not an instruction to split a tiny cohort. Recruit consenting adults through founder introductions and screened referrals. The first evidence packet should include dated cohort counts, qualitative failure cases, representation corrections, introduction outcomes, costs and member quotes used with permission.

The defensible asset may become a consented feedback system about which representations and shared experiences help people make good choices. That is a hypothesis, not a current data moat. Do not promise trained personality twins, guaranteed partners, paid conversions, market superiority or a fundraising close from this release.
